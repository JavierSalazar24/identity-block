Features

Decentralization

Blockchain-based digital identity systems are decentralized, controlled by a single central authority. Information is stored on multiple nodes inside the blockchain network, protecting it from hacking or unauthorized access.

Interoperability

Digital identity on blockchain can facilitate interoperability between diverse systems. Different organizations and applications can take advantage of the same digital identity infrastructure, enabling seamless integration and interaction while maintaining the privacy and security of user data.

Immutable Audit Trail

The blockchain maintains an immutable, tamper-proof record of identity transactions. This provides a transparent history regarding authentication, authorization and data updates. It helps establish trust and accountability.

Enhanced Security

Blockchain technology uses cryptographic techniques to protect information. This ensures that only authorized parties can access and interact with identity data, making it extremely difficult to forge or manipulate identities.

Privacy Protection

Blockchain-based digital identity systems allow users to reveal specific attributes or assertions about themselves without revealing their entire identity, helping to protect privacy and reduce the risk of misuse of information.

Trust and Verification

Blockchain-based identity systems can enhance trust and verification processes by providing a reliable source of identity information.

Self-Sovereignty

Digital identity on the blockchain enables individuals to have control and ownership over their personal information.
